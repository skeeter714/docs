## Accept your invitation

Open the invitation sent to your work email address. Each invitation works once and expires in 14 days.

- If you are new to RealClear, enter your **Full name** and set and confirm your password. Select **Accept and sign in**.
- If you already have a RealClear account, sign in with the email address that received the invitation and select **Accept invitation**. Follow any security prompts to finish.

## Sign in to RealClear

On **Sign in**, enter your address under **Team email** and select **Continue**. Enter your password and select **Sign in**, or select **Email me a sign-in link** when that option appears. If you have a passkey, select **Sign in with a passkey**. Use **Forgot your password?** to start a password reset.

If you are asked for a second factor, enter your **Authenticator code**. If you need a recovery code, select **Use a recovery code** when offered.

## Add protection in Settings

Open **Settings** and select **Account**. Under **Security**, select **Enroll two-factor** to set up an authenticator app. Privileged accounts need an authenticator before they can make certain changes.

Under **Passkeys**, select **Add passkey** when available and follow your device's prompts. You need a browser that supports passkeys and a configured authenticator fallback. Follow any prompt to sign in again before changing passkeys.

## How workspace access works

RealClear keeps each organization's reports in its own workspace. Your membership controls which organization's reports you can open. Your role controls what you can change. See [Team and settings](/team-and-settings) to learn who can manage access.

If your invitation has expired, ask an organization owner to send a new one. If you are still locked out, use [Contact RealClear](https://www.realclear.ai/contact).
