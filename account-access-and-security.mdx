title: "Account access and security"
description: "Sign in, reset access, manage your organization, and keep workspace access controlled."
---

RealClear workspaces are tied to your team account and organization. Use the account and organization settings to keep the right people in the right workspace.

## Sign in

Enter your team email on the sign-in page. You can sign in with your password or request a time-limited email sign-in link.

If you cannot access the account:

- Check that you are using the email address invited to the workspace.
- Request a fresh sign-in link; previous links expire.
- Use **Forgot your password?** to start a reset when a password is the right route.
- Contact RealClear if your organization, role, or email domain has changed.

## Account settings

Use **Settings** to review your profile and security options. You may be required to reset a password before accessing the workspace if your account’s security state calls for it.

## Organization access

Organization managers can invite collaborators and manage team roles from **Organization** settings. Give people only the level of access they need, and remove access when their involvement ends.

<Warning>
  Do not share your password, an email sign-in link, or an authenticated session. Each gives access beyond the ordinary public support site.
</Warning>

## Session and privacy basics

RealClear uses protected workspace sessions and CSRF safeguards for customer-side changes. Keep your browser and email account secured, especially on shared devices. Sign out when you finish using a shared or public computer.

## Report an access concern

If you believe an account, invite, workspace, or memo has been accessed incorrectly, contact RealClear promptly with the affected workspace and the relevant URL or memo identifier. Do not include credentials in the message.
