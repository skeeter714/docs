title: "Manage your team and workspace"
description: "Rename your workspace, invite collaborators, and manage access as an organization owner."
---

## Rename your workspace

In **Settings**, open **Account**. Under **Organization account**, select **Edit**. Update **Organization name**, then select **Save**.

## Invite a teammate

1. In **Settings**, open **Organization**. If you own more than one workspace, choose the workspace you want to manage.
2. Under **Invite collaborators to this workspace**, enter the person's **Work email** and choose a **Role**. You can add a **Personal note (optional)**.
3. Select **Send invite**. The invitation appears under **Pending invitations** until the person accepts or the invitation expires or is revoked.

### Choose a role

- **Organization owner** can manage workspace settings, invitations, and roles.
- **Deal team member** can request reports when the workspace is open for reports and review the work.
- **Read-only reviewer** can read delivered reports and their sources.

## Manage access

Under **Pending invitations**, use **Revoke invite** to cancel an invitation. Its link stops working. You can send a new invitation to that address.

Under **People with access**, use the role selector to change a member's role, or select **Remove member** to remove their access. The workspace must keep at least one owner.
