---
title: "Use report tools"
description: "Prepare for a meeting, compare delivered reports, and print or export report materials."
---

Open a delivered report and select **Report tools**.

## Prepare for a decision

- **Committee sheet and standards** puts the answer, approval routes, and first unresolved condition in a printable **Decision sheet** when the report establishes those details. Use **Landscape pages** for more detail, including standards, open questions, and source notes. Select **Download standards CSV** to work with the standards in a spreadsheet.
- **Planner questions and records requests** gathers open questions for a call with the planner. It shows what has been tried and what could answer each question when the report supplies those details. Select **Copy request** when a draft public records request is available.
- **Precedents and hearing preparation** gathers the deciding bodies, cited hearings, and questions to prepare for a hearing. It links to public records you can read before the hearing. Under **Decisions in this collection**, you may see options to search by jurisdiction or filter by deciding body.
- **Program-fit worksheet** lets you pick a saved **Program version** and compare a numeric requirement you enter with evidence in the report. You can add one other delivered report under **Compare with**. Select **Load worksheet** to open it and **Print worksheet** to print it.

## Compare reports

- **Compare a shortlist** shows up to three delivered reports from the same workspace side by side. Use it to compare proposed uses, answers, approval routes, conditions, standards, and unresolved questions. If **Shortlist** appears in **Reports**, you can open it there too.
- **Compare report editions** shows differences between two delivered reports with comparable property and use records in the same workspace. Choose a report and select **Compare reports** to see the earlier and later findings with their supporting evidence where available.

## Print or save as PDF

On the report, select **Text version and print** or **Text version (every list) and print**, whichever appears. If you are already on the text version, you can go straight to **Print or save as PDF**. In your browser's print dialog, choose a printer or **Save as PDF**. **Committee sheet and standards** has the same print button for its materials.
