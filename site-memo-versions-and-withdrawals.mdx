# Current Site Memo availability

Customers receive the **current released Site Memo** for a property. Historical edition selection is not a customer-facing feature in the current bounded release.

## The current edition is the decision material

Use the currently released memo, its citations, and its stated known unknowns when discussing the property. Do not assume that older editions can be opened, compared, downloaded, or selected from a customer version-history panel.

If the underlying position changes, RealClear will confirm the current customer-facing memo through the founder-assisted support process.

## If a memo is withdrawn

A withdrawn memo is not available as a normal buyer-facing report. Treat it as superseded and do not continue circulating it as a current position.

Withdrawal does not itself provide a replacement answer. It signals that the former customer-facing edition should not be relied upon while the underlying position is clarified.

## Requesting clarification

Contact RealClear with the site or memo name and the decision question. We will confirm the current released material and whether additional founder-assisted review is appropriate.

A Site Memo is decision support. It does not replace legal, engineering, environmental, title, lending, or other professional advice.
