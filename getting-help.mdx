## Send a support request

1. In the signed-in app, open **Settings**, then **Support**.
2. Choose the **Support workspace** for your question.
3. Under **New request**, enter a **Subject** and describe the issue under **What do you need?**
4. Select **Send request**.

If your workspace cannot send a request yet, use the public [Contact RealClear](https://www.realclear.ai/contact) page and name your organization.

Include the report link or property address if your question is about a report. Say what you were trying to do and what you saw. If your question is about evidence, include the statement number or source page. Keep passwords and recovery codes out of your message.

## Read replies

Replies appear as **RealClear support** in the request's **Conversation**. Return to **Settings**, then **Support**. Choose the same workspace and select the request under **Your requests**. Select **Refresh** to check for updates.

To add information, enter it under **Add detail for support**, then select **Post comment**.

## Contact RealClear without an account

Open the public [Contact RealClear](https://www.realclear.ai/contact) page and select **Request access** to send your inquiry.
