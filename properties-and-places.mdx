Use **New report** to ask about a property and the use you have in mind. Describe the property and proposal in your own words. The form has fields for the address and proposed use, plus two optional fields for context.

## What to enter

| Field | What it accepts |
| --- | --- |
| **Addresses** | Required. Enter one to five addresses, one per line. Each address can be up to 300 characters. The lines are part of a single report request with one proposed use. |
| **What do you want to build or do here?** | Required. Describe your proposed use in free text, up to 2,000 characters. |
| **Parcel numbers** | Optional. Enter up to 20 parcel numbers, separated by commas. Each can be up to 40 characters and can contain letters, numbers, spaces, periods, and hyphens. Include an address even if you have a parcel number. |
| **What you already know** | Optional. Enter up to 4,000 characters of background about the property or proposal. |

Find **Parcel numbers** and **What you already know** under **Add parcel numbers or background (optional)**.

## When the public record is thin

The answer depends on what the public record establishes for the address and proposed use. If the available record leaves a property detail or approval route unresolved, look for **Not established** and check **Open questions** for what could answer it.

The map depends on the public map layers available for the property. If the map could not be drawn, the report explains why. See [Evidence and quotations](/evidence-and-quotations) to understand the limits of a finding.
