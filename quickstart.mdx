---
title: "Order your first report"
description: "Request a property report in Reports and see what happens next."
---

Bring the property address and describe what you want to build or do there in your own words. If you have parcel numbers or background that helps explain your question, have those ready too.

You need a workspace with report ordering enabled and a role that can request reports.

1. Sign in to RealClear. Open **Reports** and select **New report**.
2. Enter the property address under **Addresses**. Describe your proposed use under **What do you want to build or do here?**
3. To include more context, open **Add parcel numbers or background (optional)**. Enter **Parcel numbers** separated by commas. Use **What you already know** for background about the property or your proposal.
4. Select **Start the report**. When RealClear accepts your request, the report page opens and shows its current stage.

You can close the page and open the report again from **Reports**. When it shows **Delivered**, open it from the same list.

See [Properties and places](/properties-and-places) for the form limits and [Report progress](/report-progress) for the timing and wait messages. If you need access, see [Get help](/getting-help).
