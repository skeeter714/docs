Open a delivered report from **Reports**.

## Read the answer and approval routes

Start with **The answer**. It brings together the proposed use, the conclusion, and the main findings. Where a finding has **Basis and limits**, select it for more context.

Open **Ways in** to review the approval routes the report considered. Select a route or a row for details such as **Who decides**, **Steps**, and **Strings attached**.

RealClear uses three verdict terms in its public offer.

- **Pursue** means the route is open and the decisive facts have citations.
- **Resolve first** means at least one question that could decide the route is still open. The decision names who can resolve it.
- **Pass** means the cited facts rule out every route in scope.

Read the answer on your report for the conclusion about your property and proposed use.

## Check the map

When the map is available, it appears near the answer and draws from public map layers. Use the available layer controls to see the features shown. If a message says the map is still being drawn, reload the page in a few minutes. If it could not be drawn, the report gives the reason.

## Open a quotation and its source

1. Select a numbered reference next to a finding or statement. It opens the quotation and its source details. To highlight statements with supporting references, turn on **Show the proof**.
2. Select **Open the source page** to read the original public page.
3. Go to **The proof** for **Every statement**, its quotations, and **Sources**. Use **Search** under **Every statement** to look up a word, number, or source.

If your report opens in the text version, start with **The short answer** and **Routes**. Select a numbered reference to jump to **Statements and their quotations**, then follow the source link below the quotation.

## Review open questions

In **The proof**, look for **Open questions**. The text version has its own **Open questions** section. Use the list to see what the public record leaves unresolved and what could answer it.

See [Evidence and quotations](/evidence-and-quotations) for how to read a quotation and [Report tools](/report-tools) for ways to work with the report.
