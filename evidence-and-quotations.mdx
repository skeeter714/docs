RealClear reads public records for your report. It checks quotations word for word against the pages it kept. RealClear leaves out a quotation it cannot verify against a kept page.

The statement explains the finding. The quotation gives you the source's words. Read the two together, then open the source page for more context. A passage marked **Reading** gives RealClear's interpretation. In the text version, that label is **Our reading**.

If you enter background under **What you already know**, the text version shows it as information you provided.

## What an open question means

An open question identifies something the public record leaves unresolved. Check why it is open, what RealClear tried, and what could answer it. Where the report identifies a record to request, it can name the office and the record.

**Not established** marks a gap in the evidence. Use the [report's sources](/reading-a-report#open-a-quotation-and-its-source) to see how far the record goes.

## How to use the report

Use a property report for decision support. Get legal, engineering, or other professional advice separately. Obtain any survey or approval you need through the appropriate process.

If you spot a source or statement that needs attention, [send a support request](/getting-help) with the report link and the statement number or source page.
