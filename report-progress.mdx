title: "Follow report progress"
description: "See what each report stage means and what to do if your report pauses."
---

After you select **Start the report** and RealClear accepts your request, the progress page opens. It updates automatically and shows **Public pages read so far**.

## Report stages

1. **Waiting to start**. RealClear has received your request and is waiting to begin.
2. **Researching the public record**. RealClear is looking for and reading public sources.
3. **Checking every quotation and writing the report**. RealClear is checking the quotations against the pages it kept and putting the report together.
4. **Delivered**. You can open the report.

A report takes about an hour, sometimes longer. You can close the page and select the property again in **Reports** to check progress or read the delivered report.

## Wait messages and incomplete reports

If a wait reason appears, read it for the reason your report is waiting. A message that starts **Waiting on us:** means the delay is on RealClear's side. If the message says the report will start again by itself, you can leave the page and check back.

If RealClear needs to check what happened before work can continue, the report page shows **Paused**. You can check it again in **Reports**.

A report can also arrive with less than the full answer. **Delivered: a partial report** means the research ended early. **Delivered: a limited report** means no statement could be established with a quotation that checked. Open the report to see what RealClear could establish and what remains open.

If you need help with a report, [send a support request](/getting-help).
